01 — THE DESIGN IDEA

Step beyond
the rectangle.

What if a smartphone could take on a completely different shape?

Most smartphones use a familiar rectangular body. Our concept explores an oval form with curved edges and circular components. The result is a distinctive silhouette designed with the possibility of a more comfortable, natural grip during everyday use.

04 — WHY IT MATTERS

Designed with
tomorrow in mind.

A different shape is only the beginning.

The curved body is intended to make the phone easier to hold during everyday activities. Its distinctive appearance also gives the product its own identity.

As a future design direction, the concept could use recycled materials, durable construction, and replaceable components to extend its lifespan and help reduce electronic waste.

These are proposed design goals, not tested product specifications.
